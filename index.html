⚡AI Generated Exam Summary

1. Laws of Reflection: Angle of incidence equals angle of reflection ($\angle i = \angle r$). The incident ray, normal, and reflected ray all lie in the same plane.

2. Mirror Formula: $\frac{1}{v} + \frac{1}{u} = \frac{1}{f}$, where $v$ is image distance, $u$ is object distance, and $f$ is focal length.

3. Refractive Index: $n = \frac{c}{v}$, ratio of speed of light in vacuum to speed of light in the medium.
